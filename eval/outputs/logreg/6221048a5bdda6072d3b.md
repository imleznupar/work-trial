### Do I need a minimum audience size or followers to start monetizing?

The need for a minimum audience size depends on the monetization method and the platform you use. For approaches such as selling your video content directly through a service like Vimeo Streaming, you generally aren't required to have a specific follower count — instead, the emphasis is on having a committed, engaged niche audience that is willing to pay or aligns closely with your brand's target demographic.
