## FAQ

What's the difference between Limitless Lash Mascara and Fullest Volumizing Mascara?

Limitless Lash Mascara lengthens, lifts, and separates for fluttery, fanned-out lashes. Fullest Volumizing Mascara adds weightless, buildable volume and thickness. Our founder, Sasha, loves to use Limitless during the day for a more natural look and Fullest at night for full, fluffy volume that makes a statement. Both are safe for sensitive eyes, smudge-free, flake-free, and easily removable with your normal cleanser.

Is Fullest Volumizing Mascara a tubing mascara?

Fullest Volumizing Mascara is not a tubing mascara, but it washes off easily with your normal cleanser just like one.

How do I remove Fullest Volumizing Mascara?

The smudge-free, flake-free formula washes off easily with your normal cleanser—no scrubbing, tugging, or heavy-duty makeup remover needed.

Do I need to let the formula dry between layers?

There's no need to let the formula dry between layers, but for maximum volume, you may want to wait 30 seconds between each coat.

Is Fullest Volumizing Mascara vegan?

Fullest Volumizing Mascara is cruelty-free but not vegan, due to a small amount of ethically sourced beeswax.

What can I do to prevent mascara from transferring around my eyes?

To help prevent transferring on oily or hooded lids, try setting your eye area with Soft Focus Setting Powder. We also recommend avoiding heavy creams or oils around the eyes, as this can cause mascara to transfer.
