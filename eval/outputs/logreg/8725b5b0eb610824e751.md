### Charging Tips

|  |
| --- |
| **1. Smart Device Info Display**  This charger identifies and displays the device model and battery percentage for iPhone 17 / 16 / 15 series (excluding iPhone 17e) and iPad (10th generation or later). Device identification is not supported for other devices like Samsung or Pixel, but charging will still function normally.  **2. Real-Time Wattage Display** The screen displays charging power in real time. Maximum power is determined by your device and may not reach 45W. Power will gradually decrease as the battery level increases. Lower wattage is normal. Charging speed may vary to help optimize battery health based on temperature, battery level, and usage.  **3. Charging Protocol Information**  Supports up to 45W PD fast charging, 3A PPS, and 5A PPS. Some Samsung Galaxy models—including S23+, S23 Ultra, S24+, and S24 Ultra—are limited to 27W max charging. Galaxy S25 series supports full-speed fast charging. When StandBy Mode is active, power output will be reduced. |
