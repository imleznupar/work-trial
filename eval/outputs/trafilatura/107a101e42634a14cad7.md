Margaret Hamilton, a computing pioneer whose software was key in taking the Apollo 11 astronauts to the moon, has died at age 90. Her death was announced by the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), where she worked for over a decade. According to the reports, she passed away on September 30 from an undisclosed cause. Born in Paoli, Indiana, in 1936, she studied mathematics at the University of Michigan before transferring to Earlham College.

You never know where your career will take you. From pop star to burgeoning art curator, RM of Korean pop group BTS has co-curated his first museum exhibition at the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art. RM x SFMOMA: Between You and Me consists of 178 works, 142 of which are from RM’s personal collection alongside 36 pieces from SFMOMA.

It’s the end of an era. Lionel Messi, one of the greatest soccer stars of all time, has said goodbye to his national team. Messi played his final international match with Argentina against Benin at Mas Monumental stadium in Buenos Aires, the largest venue of its kind in South America. Given the enormity of the event, the game was marked by heartfelt tributes, letting Messi know just how much he will be missed.

Head Trip is taking over the California desert. From October 10 to 11, 2026, the electronic music festival will welcome EDM juggernauts, including Calvin Harris, Swedish House Mafia, and Skrillex. It’s a one-time-only event taking over Empire Polo Club in Indio, a city in the state’s famed Coachella Valley. For avid festival-goers, the venue is the same place that hosts Coachella and Stagecoach.
