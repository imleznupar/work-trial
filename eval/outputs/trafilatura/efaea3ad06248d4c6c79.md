Our "Like-New" Gold Standard: The Inspection Process

What makes a "Rebrew" product different from "used" coffee gear found elsewhere? Every unit—whether it’s a **Stagg EKG Electric Kettle**, an **Ode Brew Grinder**, or an **Aiden Precision Coffee Maker**—is sent back to our headquarters for a rigorous 10-point restoration process:

**Deep Cleaning:** All food-safe components are sanitized to a factory-fresh state.

**Performance Calibration:** We test PID controllers for temperature accuracy and motor torque for grinding consistency.

**Component Replacement:** Any part not meeting our "Like-New" threshold is replaced with genuine Fellow components.

**Final Quality Cupping:** Our gear is validated to ensure it produces the exact flavor profile Fellow is known for.
