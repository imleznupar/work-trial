When and how often should I take Magnesium+?

We recommend taking Magnesium+ about 30–60 minutes before bedtime to support your wind-down routine. The formula is designed with magnesium to support mind and muscle relaxation and tart cherry to support sleep. Magnesium+ is designed for daily use.\*

How best should I mix Magnesium+?

Magnesium+ mixes best with 8–12 fl oz of room-temperature water. Add 2 teaspoons of Magnesium+ first, then add water, and stir well—or use a frother for an extra-smooth blend. The light berry flavor makes it a cozy part of your nighttime ritual. To note: the more water you use, the lighter and less concentrated the flavor.

Is Magnesium+ safe for pregnancy and lactation?

Yes, Magnesium+ is designed to be safe for use during pregnancy and postpartum. As always, we recommend checking with a healthcare provider.
