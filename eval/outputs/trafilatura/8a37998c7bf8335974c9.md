# Reviews

1. 
							[Neuroscience](https://www.sciencenews.org/topic/neuroscience)						 
				[*A Mother’s Brain* explores the neuroscience of motherhood](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/mommy-brain-neuroscience-book)			Neuroscientist Susana Carmona’s book guides readers through a new mom’s changing brain.
2. 
							[Plants](https://www.sciencenews.org/topic/plants)						 
				[*Happy Snacking, Don’t Die!* is a joyous guide to eating wild plants](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/happy-snacking-dont-die-cookbook)			The joy that author Alexis Nikole Nelson injects into this cookbook makes foraging approachable.
3. 
							[Ecosystems](https://www.sciencenews.org/topic/ecosystems)						 
				[*Bogland* explores beautifully strange ecosystems](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/book-review-bogland-strange-ecosystems)			Ecologist Merritt Turetsky’s devotion to bogs, fens and other fascinating water-logged spaces saturates her new book.
4. 
							[Paleontology](https://www.sciencenews.org/topic/paleontology)						 
				[*Tyrant Lizard Queen* offers a nuanced look at *T. rex*](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/tyrant-lizard-queen-t-rex-review)			Paleontologist Riley Black’s latest book explores how this misunderstood carnivore might have lived.
5. 
							[Animals](https://www.sciencenews.org/topic/animals)

6. 
							[Archaeology](https://www.sciencenews.org/topic/archaeology)

By[Shi En Kim](https://www.sciencenews.org/author/shi-en-kim)
