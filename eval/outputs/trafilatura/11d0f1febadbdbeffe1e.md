Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of Loop Switch 2?

Loop Switch 2 combines all of Loop's best hearing protection technology into one earplug. Featuring three adjustable noise-reduction modes – Quiet, Engage and Experience – Loop Switch 2 lets you control your volume throughout the day, wherever you are. It’s our only earplug that offers a convenient solution for different environments, from work to live events and more.

Loop Switch 2 has been improved for added comfort, better hearing protection and a sleeker look. The best thing? It’s fully manual, so no batteries or electronics are needed – just turn the mechanical dial for total sound control.

What settings are available on Loop Switch 2?

Loop Switch 2 features a mechanical dial with three modes to adjust your auditory environment:

Engage Mode: Lowers volume by 20 dB (SNR) while keeping speech clear for easier conversations.

Experience Mode: Reduces volume by 23 dB (SNR) while preserving crisp sound for live events.

Quiet Mode: Delivers peace and quiet with the highest noise reduction at 26 dB (SNR).

How do I adjust the noise reduction modes on Loop Switch 2?

On your Loop Switch 2 earplugs, you'll see three circles representing the different noise reduction modes and a mechanical dial. To adjust, simply rotate the dial to align with the desired mode:

Engage Mode: Rotate to the thin circle at the front. This mode reduces volume by 20 dB (SNR), filtering background noise while keeping conversations clear.

Experience Mode: Turn to the half-filled circle in the center. This mode lowers volume by 23 dB (SNR) while maintaining sound quality.

Quiet Mode: Rotate to the filled circle at the back for the strongest noise reduction at 26 dB (SNR).

Note: You can switch modes directly in-ear and will feel a gentle 'click' when the mode is selected.

Can I sleep with Loop Switch 2?

Loop Switch isn’t designed for sleeping. They’re bulkier than other models, so they may not be the most comfortable for sleeping.

For earplugs designed for sleep, Loop Dream is your best option. These earplugs offer maximum sleep comfort, powerful noise reduction and all-night security – even for side sleepers.

What do Loop’s decibel ratings actually mean?

The dB (or decibel) rating tells you how much quieter things get when you wear them. The higher the number, the more sound is reduced.

Switch doesn't have one fixed dB rating – it has three.

Engage Mode runs at 20 dBs reduction: for conversations, social situations, and anywhere you want to take the edge off without losing the room.

Experience Mode runs at 23 dBs reduction: for concerts, events, and louder settings where you want protection without losing the energy of what you're hearing.

Quiet Mode runs at 26 dBs reduction: maximum reduction for commutes, studying, or any moment you need proper quiet.

The rating doesn't change the earplug, just the dial. Switch lets you match your protection to what you're doing right now, without carrying a different pair for every situation.

For a full breakdown of each product's dB rating, read What level of sound reduction does Loop Earplugs offer?

Dream and Quiet are designed to block as much sound as possible. Experience and Engage are designed to let a quieter version of sound through – so music still sounds like music and voices stay clear. Their lower ratings are the point.

The rating is measured in a lab, with a perfect fit. In real life, how much reduction you get depends on wearing them correctly: the right ear tip size, inserted properly.
