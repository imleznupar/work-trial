### Frequent Questions

When do you take Fertility Support?

Fertility Support is a clean, traceable, 3-in-1 formula designed to support conception outcomes and the health of your eggs. It can be taken prior to trying-to-conceive, prior to a fertility event, and while actively trying-to-conceive. We recommend starting three months prior to conception or related fertility milestones. It can be safely taken even before you know you're pregnant.*

Should I be taking Essential Prenatal alongside Fertility Support when TTC?

We see Fertility Support and Essential Prenatal Multivitamin as complementary products when trying to conceive; they are designed to be taken together. Our 3-in-1 Fertility Support is formulated with three ingredients — CoQ10, NAC, and Myo-Inositol — to support fertility health. We recommend taking Fertility Support with Essential Prenatal, which supports adequate intake of key nutrients like Folate, Omega-3 DHA, and Choline to support both mom and baby during preconception and pregnancy. These two products work hand-in-hand to support fertility health while also providing key essential nutrient support during this lifestage.* While you can continue taking Essential Prenatal multivitamin throughout your pregnancy, we recommend not taking Fertility Support beyond a positive pregnancy test.*

Why did you choose a powder?

Our formulation contains clinically-studied amounts of active ingredients, including CoQ10, NAC, and Myo-Inositol, within a blend of about 4g per serving scoop. Each of our ingredients has been integrated into the formula at its clinically studied dosage when taken twice per day, and we've chosen the powder form to ensure that you receive these clinically studied dosages effectively.

How often should I take Fertility Support?

Drink twice daily, once in the morning and once at night, for ingredient dosing backed by clinical trials. You can enjoy Fertility Support with or without food. We recommend starting three months prior to a fertility milestone (including trying to conceive).*

Why are there floating particles of yellow?

That’s our clinically-studied bioavailable CoQ10. CoQ10 is an ingredient that supports cellular energy production and is clinically studied to help support follicular health. Even if it doesn’t look dissolved, as with all Ritual products, you can trust that what you’re reading on the label is what you’re getting, so we’ve intentionally included enough CoQ10 in the formula, designed to help ensure you are getting the dose as promised, even if there are yellow particles left behind after drinking.*

What testing do you complete for Fertility Support?

We go above and beyond when it comes to third-party testing because we believe you deserve to know what you’re putting in your body and why. Third-party testing helps ensure our products comply with regulations and guidelines for heavy metals, microbes, and major allergens and assures the quality and safety of our products. Fertility Support has been independently certified for quality and purity. It is Non-GMO Project Verified and earned the Purity Award from the Clean Label Project.

What can I expect while taking Fertility Support?

While you may not feel a difference taking Fertility Support while trying to conceive, it’s what’s happening inside that counts. CoQ10 and NAC provide cell-protectant properties to support an ideal environment for healthy eggs, and Myo-inositol promotes normal ovulation cycles and helps support healthy hormone levels and egg health. If you have any questions about your personal supplementation, we suggest consulting your healthcare professional or physician.*
