## Cleansers, Toners & Exfoliators FAQ

What’s the difference between a toner and a cleanser?

A cleanser, like Beekman 1802’s Milk Wash Exfoliating Jelly Cleanser, is used to remove dirt, oil, and impurities by cleaning the surface of the skin, removing makeup, and unclogging pores. A toner is typically applied to the skin after cleansing to aid in balancing the pH level of the skin and removing any remaining traces of impurities. Beekman 1802’s Milk Shake Hyaluronic Acid & Squalane Facial Toner Mist utilizes a combination of ultra nourishing goat milk and mugwort, plus squalane and hyaluronic acid to keep skin both toned and hydrated all day.

Should I tone before or after cleansing?

Typically, toning is done after cleansing. After cleansing your face with a cleanser, you can use a toner like Beekman 1802’s Milk Shake Hyaluronic Acid & Squalane Facial Toner Mist to remove any leftover impurities, balance the pH level of your skin, and prepare skin for the next steps in your skincare routine.

Should I use a toner before moisturizing?

Yes! Toner helps to prepare your skin for better absorption of the moisturizer and other products that you may apply afterward. For more intensive hydration, try an ultra-hydrating toner like Beekman 1802’s Milk Shake Hyaluronic Acid & Squalane Facial Toner Mist and follow with Beekman 1802’s rich Midnight Milk Better Aging Sleep Mask to restore the skin’s barrier for a more youthful complexion.

What’s the difference between a chemical exfoliant and a physical exfoliant?

A chemical exfoliant, like Beekman 1802’s Milk Wash Exfoliating Jelly Cleanser, uses chemicals like alpha hydroxy acids (AHAs) and beta hydroxy acids (BHAs) to promote cellular turnover and reveal smoother, brighter skin. A physical exfoliant involves manually scrubbing the skin to remove dead skin cells through the use of granules, like the gentle jojoba beads found in Beekman 1802’s Milk Scrub Oat & Goat Milk Exfoliating Cleanser.

Why is goat milk important for facial cleansing?

Goat milk is known for its gentle nature, which can help calm and soothe even the most sensitive skin. Because it’s rich in vitamins and minerals, as well as lactic acid which acts as a mild exfoliant, Beekman 1802’s Milk Scrub Oat & Goat Milk Exfoliating Cleanser utilizes this super ingredient to deeply cleanse skin while leaving it feeling hydrated and radiant.
