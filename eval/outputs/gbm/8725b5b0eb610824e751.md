## FAQ

### Why does the screen turn off automatically instead of staying on?

To reduce light pollution and extend the screen's lifespan, it automatically turns off after 1 minute of inactivity.

### Why don't some devices show battery level or model on the screen?

Only iPhone 15 and later models and iPad (10th generation) and later support battery level and model identification. Other devices, such as Samsung Galaxy phones, Google Pixel phones, and tablets, can still charge normally but may not support these display features. 2-in-1 cables are not compatible with model identification.

### Why do some devices show only a generic model name instead of the full model name?

Update your device to the latest software version and try again. If the full model name still does not appear, this is normal and does not affect charging performance.

### Why is the charging power lower than expected, or why is charging slower than usual?

First, make sure you haven't accidentally double-tapped to activate Care Mode. Charging power automatically adjusts based on your device's needs, with a maximum output of up to 45W.

Some devices use proprietary charging protocols that may limit charging speed. Charging power is typically highest when the battery level is low and gradually decreases as the battery fills.

### How do I update the charger so it recognizes the latest iPhone models?

① Install the Anker app. ② Connect the charger using a USB cable, then tap the device card in the app. ③ Tap "Update" and keep the charger connected. ④ When the update is complete, the charger will restart automatically.

### Why doesn't my device name appear on the display?

This feature is optimized for iPhone 15 and later models. On older iPhones or other devices, the specific model name may not appear. This affects the display only and does not affect normal charging.

### Why doesn't the battery level appear for my older iPhone with a Lightning port?

Real-time battery level display requires USB-C communication supported by iPhone 15 and later models. Older iPhones with Lightning ports can still fast charge, but the screen will not display the battery percentage.

### Is it normal for the charger to get warm while charging?

Yes, this is normal. Some warmth during fast charging is expected. Built-in Care Mode and intelligent temperature control help keep charging temperatures within international safety limits for everyday use.
