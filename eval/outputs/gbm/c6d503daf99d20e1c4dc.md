### How does this chino fit compared to a standard chino?

The Stretch Twill Chino has a mid-rise fit that's fitted through the hip and thigh with a slightly tapered leg opening, so it sits higher than a typical chino but won't hug your legs like a slim fit would. If you're between sizes, size down for the best fit.

### What's the fabric actually like to wear?

You're getting cotton cavalry twill that's naturally soft and gets even softer the more you wear and wash it. The spandex blend adds just enough stretch to move with you without feeling flimsy, and the midweight construction holds its shape for both office days and casual wear.

### Is the inseam accurate to size?

Pay attention to your actual inseam measurement. Multiple customers have reported that the inseam runs long, so confirm your length before ordering. The model is 6'2" wearing a 32X32, which you can use as a reference.

### Can I machine wash these, and will they shrink?

Machine wash cold with like colors and tumble dry low. The cotton composition means some shrinkage is possible, so following the cold wash and low dry instructions will help minimize it.

### What makes the hidden pocket useful?

The stealth zip pocket on the side lets you secure smaller items like keys, cards, or your phone separately from your regular pockets, keeping everything organized and protected without any visible bulk.
