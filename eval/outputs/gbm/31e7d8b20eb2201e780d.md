# Simply Co. Laundry Detergent

###### Overview

This is hands down the simplest laundry detergent on the market.

Our concentrated powder formula has just 3 natural ingredients (4 if you choose scented!) that are safe, effective, biodegradable, and plastic-free. One 32 oz. jar offers a non-toxic, sustainable clean for up to 60 loads of laundry. Plus, the glass jar can be reused over and over!

Safe and gentle for households with babies, kids, and pets. Allergen friendly. HE and Septic Safe.

###### Ingredients

Unscented: Sodium Cocoate, Sodium Bicarbonate, Sodium Carbonate

Lavender: Sodium Cocoate, Sodium Bicarbonate, Sodium Carbonate, Lavender Essential Oil (Contains Linalyl Acetate)

###### How to use

Use 1 tbsp of 3-Ingredient Laundry Detergent for small loads, and 2 tbsp for large loads.

For top loading machines: Put 1-2 tbsp of detergent into the empty drum of your machine. Load your clothes and wash as normal.

For front loading machines: Put 1-2 tbsp of detergent into the detergent compartment of your machine's drawer. Load your clothes and wash as normal.
