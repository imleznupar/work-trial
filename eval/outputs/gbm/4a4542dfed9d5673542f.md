## Glacial Mint & Eucalyptus Bodycare FAQs

Does eucalyptus lotion repel mosquitoes?

Eucalyptus oil is shown to repel mosquitoes because the strong smell can interfere with mosquitoes’ senses. So, with enough eucalyptus lotion, you may find fewer mosquito bites!

Can I use eucalyptus lotion while pregnant?

Eucalyptus essential oil isn’t recommended for those pregnant or breastfeeding, however, our eucalyptus lotion is made with My Z-Biome Fragrance Technology which is safe for sensitive skin. If you’re pregnant, we recommend confirming with your doctor before trying new products.

How to make eucalyptus soap?

Our eucalyptus soap is made with goat milk, shea butter, jojoba oil, and other microbiome-friendly ingredients so you can enjoy a deep cleanse and non-irritating formula. Check out our other goat milk soap for more intoxicating scents.

What is eucalyptus soap good for?

Our eucalyptus soap is good for deeply cleansing, hydrating and softening your skin, without stripping it of its natural oils and moisture. Our eucalyptus soap is non-irritating and triple-milled for the ultimate shower experience.

Does mint lip balm plump your lips?

Peppermint essential oil can offer a natural plumping effect when used in the formulation of mint lip balm. And you’re in luck! Our goat milk mint lip balm is made with sunflower seed oil, beeswax, peppermint oil, coconut oil, and goat milk for plumper, more moisturized lips.
