## FAQs

Is there an additional fee to use performance reviews?

Performance reviews are included with our Premium plan. To see all of our plans and the features they include, check out our pricing page.

I don’t have a background in HR or payroll. Will Gusto work for me?

Gusto is designed with both pros and first-time users in mind. We’ve automated as much as possible because we know you already have a lot on your plate. If you have any questions, accessible support is just a call, chat, or email away.

Are performance reviews right for my business?

Using Gusto for performance reviews is great if you currently have a manual process for facilitating performance reviews and want to automate your workflow. Some businesses might have more complex needs like OKR/goal management, weekly check-ins, or employee recognition. In those cases, we integrate with best-in-class performance management services so no one’s left hanging. Learn more about our integrations here.

What types of reviews are supported?

We currently support manager reviews (e.g. managers complete performance reviews for direct reports) and self-evaluations (e.g. direct reports complete a self-evaluation). In the future, we plan on supporting 360 feedback (e.g. direct reports complete a performance review for managers) and peer feedback (e.g. direct reports request a performance review from peers) as well.
