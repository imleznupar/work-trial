## Answers to common video monetization questions

### Talk to Sales

### Do I need a minimum audience size or followers to start monetizing?

The need for a minimum audience size depends on the monetization method and the platform you use. For approaches such as selling your video content directly through a service like Vimeo Streaming, you generally aren't required to have a specific follower count — instead, the emphasis is on having a committed, engaged niche audience that is willing to pay or aligns closely with your brand's target demographic.

### Which formats and devices are supported?

Vimeo supports a wide range of formats and devices to maximize audience engagement. Preferred video formats include H.264, Apple ProRes, and H.265 (HEVC), with resolutions up to 8K, HDR, and Dolby Vision. Viewers can watch monetized content on nearly any modern device — via web browsers, mobile apps, AirPlay, or Chromecast. Creators using Vimeo OTT can also launch branded apps on major connected TV platforms like Apple TV, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, and Smart TVs for a full multi-platform experience.

Fees differ based on the service you choose. With video on demand (VOD), which enables direct sales and rentals, you retain a certain percentage of the revenue after transaction fees are taken out. Other monetization methods, like subscription-based transactions, will charge a fee per subscriber, a percentage commission, or a flat fee for one-time purchases and merchant processing fees. Ensure you understand any video upload limits or bandwidth overage risks.

Vimeo lets you keep an unprecedented 90% of your revenue after transaction fees, maximizing your earnings.

Vimeo Streaming and Vimeo On Demand both support video sales and rentals. For subscription-based recurring revenue, live streaming, or distributing your content on apps, Vimeo Streaming is your best option.

Niche series, educational videos, and exclusive content such as behind-the-scenes access and premium experiences tend to perform well on Vimeo.
