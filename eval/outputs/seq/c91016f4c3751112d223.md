## FAQ

What is the difference between workflow and automation?

Workflow refers to the series of tasks that make up a process, while automation involves using technology to perform those tasks with minimal human intervention.

What are the 3 basic components of a workflow?

Input, process, and output. Input includes the resources needed, the process is the series of tasks performed, and output is the result. A workflow automation platform like Wrike can help you manage all these components in one place.

What is process flow automation?

Process flow automation means automating tasks to improve efficiency. It’s a key part of modern workflow management.

What are the benefits of workflow automation?

The benefits of workflow automation include increased efficiency, reduced errors, time savings, and enhanced productivity.

Does Wrike have automation?
