### How to send a password securely

Bitwarden Send empowers teams and individuals with a secure, simple way to share information directly with another person. Follow these quick steps to get started.

### 1. Create a send

Log into your Bitwarden account and click the Send tab. Choose whether you're sending Text (for passwords and messages) or a File. Enter your password or upload your document, then add a name for your Send item.

### 2. Privacy options and expiry

Enhance your secure password sharing with custom privacy settings:

Set an expiration date (from 1 hour to 30 days) for temporary password sharing

Add a password for an extra layer of protection

Limit the maximum access count

Enable "Destruct-on-Open" functionality to ensure one-time viewing only

### 3. Share the link safely

Once created, you'll receive a secure link to share. Copy this link and send it to your recipient through your preferred communication channel. For added security, consider sending the access password separately.

With true end-to-end encryption, only you and your recipient can access the content of your Send. Even Bitwarden cannot view your shared passwords or files, ensuring maximum privacy and security.
