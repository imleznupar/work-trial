## Explore easy-to-use features

Our goal is simple: to eliminate the friction between planning, doing, and delivering work. See how our features can speed up your workflow.

### Choose the view you want

Want to see your work in a familiar spreadsheet format without opening Excel or Google Sheets? Choose Table view. More of a Kanban fan? Switch over to Board view.

Wrike allows you to toggle back and forth between different views — it’s as easy as one click.

Gantt charts

### Drag and drop tasks with ease

Use our interactive Gantt chart tool to map out the direction of your project, including task durations and milestones.

Need to adjust a date or deadline? No problem — simply drag and drop to update.

Dashboards

### Create dashboards in seconds

With Wrike, you can build custom dashboards with easy-to-configure widgets. Choose your filters (e.g., assignee, due date), so you only see the data you want.

In a rush? Get started with our prebuilt dashboard templates — no coding required.

Request forms

### Centralize all incoming requests

Build your own request form to simplify your team’s intake process. Tip: Conditional questions ensure you get all the info you need the first time around.

No time to create one from scratch? Build a custom form fast with natural-language prompts.
