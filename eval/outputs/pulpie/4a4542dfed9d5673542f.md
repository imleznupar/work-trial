Eucalyptus oil is shown to repel mosquitoes because the strong smell can interfere with mosquitoes’ senses. So, with enough **eucalyptus lotion** , you may find fewer mosquito bites!

Can I use eucalyptus lotion while pregnant? 

Eucalyptus essential oil isn’t recommended for those pregnant or breastfeeding, however, our eucalyptus lotion is made with My Z-Biome Fragrance Technology which is safe for [sensitive skin](https://beekman1802.com/collections/sensitive-skin). Does mint lip balm plump your lips?
