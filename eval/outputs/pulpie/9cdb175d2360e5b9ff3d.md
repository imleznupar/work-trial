## Frequently Asked Questions

### **What inspired Protein Oatmeal?**

We wanted to prove that oatmeal doesn’t have to be bland or boring. We set out to create a warm, creamy, protein-packed breakfast that actually tastes incredible - and after a lot of recipe testing, we finally did it.

### **What’s the best way to enjoy Protein Oatmeal?**

Two ways! Make it hot - empty the packet into a microwave-safe bowl, add up to ⅔ cup of milk or water, microwave on HIGH for 45–60 seconds, stir, and let stand for 1–2 minutes before digging in. Or make it as overnight oats - empty the packet into a bowl or jar, add up to ⅔ cup of cold milk or water, stir, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. Either way, breakfast is sorted.

### **How much sugar is in this product?**

Just 1g of total sugar per serving - deliciously sweet without the crash.

### **Is this product gluten free?**

Yes! We use certified gluten-free oats in our Protein Oatmeal.

### **I’m allergic to nuts, can I eat this?**

While this product is nut-free, it is manufactured in a facility that also processes tree nuts and sesame. If you have a severe allergy, we’d recommend checking with your doctor before trying.

### **What flavors do you offer?**

We currently offer three delicious flavors - Maple Brown Sugar, Apple Cinnamon, and Banana Bread. Each one is warm, creamy, and seriously craveable.

### **What makes Magic Spoon Protein Oatmeal so good?**

Most oatmeal is high in sugar and low in protein - we flipped that. With 15g of protein, 6g of fiber, and just 1g of sugar per serving, our Protein Oatmeal delivers the nutrition your morning deserves without sacrificing taste. Made with gluten-free oats, flaxseeds, chia seeds, and complete dairy protein - no artificial flavors, colors, or preservatives, ever.
