## How to Use  
 _Jet Lag Eye Patches_

Apply to clean, dry undereye area. Leave on for 10 minutes, or longer if desired. Discard after use and pat remaining serum into skin. Use daily, or as needed.

![SummerFridays beauty media](//summerfridays.com/cdn/shop/files/PDP-How-To-Use-JLM.jpg?v=1729019024&width=750)

## How to Use  
 _Jet Lag Mask + Moisturizer_

To use as a daytime or nighttime moisturizer, massage a lighter layer into your skin and leave on.

To use as a mask, apply a thin, even layer to clean, dry skin. After leaving on for at least 10 minutes, remove any excess with a warm cloth.

To use as an overnight mask, apply a thin, even layer to clean, dry skin before bed and leave on.

![SummerFridays beauty media](//summerfridays.com/cdn/shop/files/PDP-How-To-Use-JL-Mist.jpg?v=1729031922&width=750)

## How to Use  
 _Jet Lag Skin Soothing Hydration Mist_

With eyes closed, hold the product 12 in/30 cm away from the face and gently mist. Use as the first step in your skincare routine, after makeup application, or as needed throughout the day.

![SummerFridays beauty media](//summerfridays.com/cdn/shop/files/PDP-How-To-Use-JL-Serum.jpg?v=1729030502&width=750)

## How to Use  
 _Jet Lag Deep Hydration Serum_

Smooth over cleansed skin before applying any other targeted treatments, followed by your favorite moisturizer.
