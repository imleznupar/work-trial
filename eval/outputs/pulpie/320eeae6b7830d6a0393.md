### Break down projects into tasks and subtasks

Tackle overwhelming projects efficiently by splitting them into bite-sized subtasks in Wrike. Assign smaller tasks to different team members to distribute workload evenly and make large requests more feasible.

### Automate subtask creation with AI

Extract key action items from unstructured texts, documents, lists, and meeting notes, and organize them into subtasks with Wrike’s powerful Work Intelligence® solution.  


### Customize your workflow

Organize your tasks and projects however you like with Wrike’s subtasks, making it easy for everyone on the team to understand the project’s status. Use custom workflows to assign subtasks to different teams.  


### Monitor progress with subtasks

Get precise insights into your project’s progress using subtasks. With real-time understanding of the development status for each project, you can make accurate decisions and timely adjustments.  


### Boost collaboration and communication

Enable targeted communication, discuss specific work items, attach files, and generate conversations related to each subtask separately, reducing room for misunderstandings.  


### Simplify navigation with Wrike’s cross-tagging

Use cross-tagging to associate subtasks with multiple folders or projects, offering a comprehensive view of a task’s presence across different projects. Working cross-functionally is a breeze with Wrike.
