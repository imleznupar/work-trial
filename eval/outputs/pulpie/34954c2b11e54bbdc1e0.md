# Donate to The Paris Review

![Revel](/dist/theme/issue_257/hadada-hover.jpg)

_The Paris Review_ is not only the preeminent literary quarterly in America but also a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations help us pay the writers and artists whose work fills our pages, as well as cover our general operating and printing costs, which are constantly rising.

If you have any questions about donating to the _Review_ , please contact Jane Breakell, Development Director: [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#cea4acbcabafa5aba2a28ebaa6abbeafbca7bdbcabb8a7abb9e0a1bca9).

The _Review_ also welcomes planned giving. By leaving a cash bequest in your will, or designating the _Review_ as a beneficiary of your trust, retirement plan, or life insurance policy, you can support future issues and projects. Our legal name is The Paris Review Foundation, Inc., our EIN is 13-4081729, and our contact phone number is 212-343-1333.

Additional ways to support the _Review_ include purchasing Spring Revel [tickets](http://theparisreview.org/revel), [limited-edition prints](https://parisreviewprints.org/), and [subscriptions](https://ssl.drgnetwork.com/flex/TPR/MAIN/?utm_source=subbutton&utm_medium=site&utm_campaign=default_sub).

For a list of current institutional supporters, please visit our [Institutional Support](https://www.theparisreview.org/institutional-support) page.
