# Rep. Max Miller’s Girlfriend Was Just Indicted

## A grand jury formally charged Jaclyn Alatrash, the congressman’s partner of nearly two years, with grand theft. 

![Photo of Max Miller in doorway.](https://www.motherjones.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GettyImages-2292557736-2.jpg?w=990)

Rep. Max Miller, R-Ohio, is seen after House votes in the U.S. Capitol on Tuesday, September 1, 2026. Tom Williams—Getty Images

On Thursday, the woman Rep. Max Miller (R-Ohio) has been dating for nearly two years was indicted by a grand jury on three criminal counts:****  
grand theft, telecommunications fraud, and misuse of a credit card.

The charges against Jaclyn Alatrash, first reported by _Mother Jones,_ are in connection to allegations from her ex-husband that Alatrash used his personal credit card to purchase more than $9,000 worth of items on Instacart after they legally separated. The ex-husband, Matthew Kampert, told _Mother Jones_ that Alatrash was never an authorized user on his credit card, even when they were married.

_The Rooster_ , a progressive blog in Ohio, first [reported](https://www.rooster.info/p/max-miller-jaclyn-alatrash-credit-card-fraud-allegations) on these allegedly fraudulent Instacart charges in May. Instacart records obtained by _Mother Jones_ show that many of the deliveries went to Miller’s Northeast Ohio residence. These charges included $50.99 from Spirit Halloween, $107.31 from Ulta Beauty, $152.08 from the Vitamin Shoppe, and $207.81 from Costco, among many others.

A police report from the incident charges that Miller’s girlfriend had used Kampert’s credit card from September 2025 to January 2026. Kampert told Shaker Heights police that the dissolution of their marriage was finalized in February 2025—seven months before Alatrash allegedly started using his credit card.

Miller’s bid for a third term has already been rocked by [allegations](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2026/07/assault-misogyny-verbal-threats-records-detail-new-allegations-against-rep-max-miller-bernie-moreno/) from his ex-wife, Emily Moreno, who accused him of throwing hot water at her, shoving her, and pointing a gun at her. After these accusations were reported by _Mother Jones_ and other outlets, Moreno’s father and fellow Ohio Republican, US Sen Bernie Moreno, said Miller “should not serve in the House of Representatives” and needed “serious psychological help.” Miller has repeatedly denied ever being violent towards women.

During a sworn deposition related to Miller’s ongoing child custody dispute with Moreno, he [testified](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28715979-rep-max-miller-september-2026-deposition-redacted/?q=december&mode=document#document/p75) that he has been dating Alatrash since December 2024. In an interview that was included as a court exhibit in their custody case, Miller previously [called](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2026/08/legal-filings-allege-rep-max-miller-gave-false-statements-to-ohio-court-emily-moreno/) Alatrash a “very nice lady.” Miller and his lawyer Larry Zukerman—who has also represented Alatrash in a separate matter—did not immediately responded to questions about the indictment from _Mother Jones._

Alatrash, a Cleveland-area anesthesia assistant and body builder, is scheduled to be arraigned in Cuyahoga County, Ohio on October 23.
