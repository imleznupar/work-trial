## IAM for Government 

Government agencies are often held back by paper-based workflows, static PDFs, and disconnected systems, what we like to call the “Agreement Trap.” The Docusign Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM) is an AI-powered platform that helps you work faster, smarter, and together by transforming agreements into actionable data and aligning workflows across your departments.

## Featured Docusign products for government

  * ### [IAM Core](/solutions/iam-core)

Unleash the power of agreements with AI-powered data extraction, unlimited eSignature sends and a central repository.

  * ### [IAM for Customer Experience](/solutions/departments/customer-experience)

Improve customer experiences and conversions with prefilled forms, signing from any device, and signer identity verification.

  * ### [IAM for Sales](/solutions/departments/sales)

Accelerate the sales lifecycle with AI-powered analytics, simplified renewal tracking, and integrations with Salesforce and other CRMs.

## US Public Sector Compliance

Docusign products are designed to meet government customers’ unique security and compliance requirements.

  * ### DoD Impact Level 4 (IL4)

Docusign was granted an IL4 provisional authorization for Docusign eSignature and Docusign CLM.

  * ### FedRAMP Moderate Impact Level

Docusign IAM platform, Docusign eSignature, and Docusign CLM are FedRAMP-authorized.

  * ### GovRAMP

Docusign IAM platform, Docusign eSignature, and Docusign CLM are GovRAMP-authorized.
